# Changelog

Each version is a release tagged `german-x.y.z` on the [releases page](https://github.com/ntfifwyb/anki-setup/releases). Major: importing it needs a step in Anki. Minor: new cards. Patch: fixes.

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- The deck and its subdecks are named in German, for immersion: `Languages::Deutsch`, with `Verben` and `Ausdrücke`. The file is now `Deutsch.apkg`.
- **Before importing**, rename the decks in Anki: `Languages::German` → `Languages::Deutsch`, `Verbs` → `Verben`, `Expressions` → `Ausdrücke`. Otherwise your existing cards stay in the old decks.
- The cards themselves are unchanged.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-22)

- First release: 2,508 notes in `Languages::German`, with audio. **Verbs** (1,994): the 2,000 most common verbs, with a second meaning for 174 of them. **Expressions** (514): chunks for B2→C1 in 6 subdecks by type.
