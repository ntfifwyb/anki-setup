# Refranes

173 refranes, from the ones everyone knows but half-remembers to less common ones worth having. Part of the [Español deck](../), in `Languages::Español::01 Refranes`.

## Card

Note type **Spanish Refrán**, one card per note (*Completar*):

- **Front:** the first part of the refrán, the rest gapped, with a word count. You complete it and say what it means.
- **Back:** the whole refrán (spoken), its meaning, its German (`DE`) and English (`EN`) equivalent where a real one exists, and notes (variants, origin).

Regional refranes carry a chip and a `region::` tag, for example *El que quiere celeste, que le cueste* (Río de la Plata).

## Editing

One refrán per line of `refranes.txt`: `refrán with {{the part to complete}} | meaning | German | English | notes | region`. `region` is empty (general), `esp`, `am`, `rioplatense` or `mex`. Leave an equivalent empty rather than force one: a loose translation teaches the wrong proverb. Then rebuild as in the [deck README](../README.md#editing-and-rebuilding).
