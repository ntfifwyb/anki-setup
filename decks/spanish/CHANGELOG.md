# Changelog

Each version is a release tagged `spanish-x.y.z` on the [releases page](https://github.com/ntfifwyb/anki-setup/releases). Major: importing it needs a step in Anki. Minor: new cards. Patch: fixes.

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- The deck is named in Spanish, for immersion: `Languages::Español` (its subdecks already were). The file is now `Espanol.apkg`.
- **Before importing**, rename `Languages::Spanish` to `Languages::Español` in Anki. Otherwise your existing cards stay in the old deck.
- The cards themselves are unchanged.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- First release: 676 notes (810 cards) in `Languages::Spanish`, with audio: Refranes, Locuciones, Colocaciones, Vocabulario culto and Norma y dudas.
